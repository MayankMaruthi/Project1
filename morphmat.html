MorphMat

Build. Play. Transform.

MorphMat is a modular toy that kids can connect together to create completely different structures.

The same pieces can become a car, airplane, house, fort or other creation. When playtime is finished, everything transforms back into a floor mat.

One Toy. Many Possibilities.

1

Floor Mat

Start with the modular pieces connected as a useful play mat.

2

Build

Separate and connect the panels into a new structure.

3

Play

Create a car, airplane, house, fort or something completely new.

4

Transform

Take everything apart and reconnect the pieces into the floor mat.

Floor Mat → Build → Play → Transform → Repeat
