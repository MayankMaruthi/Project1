RCArena

Drive the Game.

RCArena combines RC cars with a physical board game. Players control miniature RC cars inside an enclosed, customizable arena.

Instead of simply driving an RC car around, players have objectives, competition and multiple game modes they can play with friends.

Choose Your Game

1

Car Soccer

Drive your RC car into the ball and score against the other team.

2

Racing

Rearrange the arena into a miniature racing circuit.

3

Capture the Flag

Race to capture objectives while defending your side of the arena.

4

Obstacle Course

Add ramps, barriers and obstacles and compete for the fastest time.

Choose Game → Set Up Arena → Drive → Compete
